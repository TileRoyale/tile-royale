Shadow Tile 🔒 Level 5
Next round all others see 2 tiles — one real, one trap that locks 1s. You see only the real tile. Usable while more than 3 players remain.

Pepper Spray 🔒 Level 8
4 tiles flash bright red for 2 seconds — fake burning tiles that confuse everyone. Usable while more than 4 players remain.
1× · 6+ players

Muscle Relaxant 🔒 Level 12
Half the players must double-tap the burning tile. First tap just dims the tile — second tap counts. Tile looks normal until first tap.
1× · 6+ players
Items activate automatically during the game. Crystal Ball activates every round. Caltrops activate on first tap. Shadow Tile activates on first tap when >3 players remain. Pepper Spray activates on first tap when >4 players remain.
